Privacy Policy

How TEZ HAI? handles your information on Android and iOS.

Draft updated: 23 October 2026 · Ayushi Srivastava

Preview — publisher review is pending. This draft is not the final policy for public release.

Who runs TEZ HAI?

TEZ HAI? (com.tezhai.app) is published by Ayushi Srivastava. For support, privacy questions, or requests about your information, email ayushis2k10@gmail.com.

Local play and cloud accounts

You can play without creating an account. Your nickname, puzzle progress, unfinished boards, settings and recorded play time are saved on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data can remove local information that has not been synced.

If you create a cloud account, we process an account identifier, username or sign-in identity, age band and any guardian-permission declaration, completed puzzle records, scores, skips, hints, mistakes, recorded play time and completion dates. We record the latest online account activity and security/session information needed to operate the account. Exact age and date of birth are not saved as profile fields.

Supabase provides authentication and cloud storage. Username accounts use an internal sign-in address, not a recovery email. Passwords are handled by the authentication service; plaintext passwords are not stored with game results. Google sign-in shares the identity information needed to authenticate you, which may include your email address, name and profile information. Username password recovery by email is currently unavailable.

Completed local results may be imported into an eligible cloud account. Unfinished boards remain on the device. Cloud saving does not promise to resume the same unfinished board on every device.

Players under 18

Players under 13 use a device-only profile. Players aged 13–17 may create a cloud account after declaring parent or guardian permission and supervision; otherwise they can play locally. Adults can create an independent cloud account. A declaration is not identity verification or verified parental consent.

Age is requested when creating or connecting an account, rather than before local play. Child and teen local journeys are separated from adult/guest journeys. Optional analytics, replay, advertising and reminders are disabled for under-18 accounts. Sharing is initiated manually through the device share sheet; review the card and destination before sending it. Contact us about a child’s information or an account that should not have been created.

Reminders and optional measurement

Eligible adult players can choose to enable device notifications and set a reminder schedule. We process the device push token, platform, account/session association, notification permission and schedule to deliver reminders. Apple Push Notification service delivers iOS notifications. Availability depends on the app build, device permission and configured sender.

Reminder measurement is a separate, optional choice that is off by default. It counts accepted sends and taps using device-linked receipts in the account database for up to 30 days. Receipts are removed when measurement or reminders are turned off, or the associated session is revoked. Campaign totals can be exported to PostHog without player IDs, push tokens or receipt IDs. Aggregated reports can remain after linked records are removed.

This React Native version does not initialize gameplay analytics, session replay or advertising SDKs, and does not send age-screen analytics. The app contains no advertisements, paid subscriptions or in-app purchases. Earlier beta versions may have collected opted-in measurements under their notices; contact us to request removal of information associated with those versions.

Sharing and device access

When an eligible player chooses to share, the app creates a result image and opens the operating system’s share sheet. You choose the destination and what to send. The receiving app’s practices apply to information you share; copies retained by recipients are outside our control.

The game plays audio but does not record your microphone. Photos access may be requested by the sharing functionality when you explicitly choose a related action. Notification permission is requested only when you enable reminders.

Service providers and technical information

Supabase processes account and game records. Google processes Google sign-in information. Apple processes iOS push delivery. PostHog may receive the optional reminder campaign aggregates described above. GitHub Pages serves these public information pages. The legal site does not include analytics, replay, advertising, account login or tracking cookies.

Network providers necessarily process information such as IP addresses, request times and technical logs to deliver and secure requests. Providers may process information outside your country. Their security logs and backups follow their applicable service terms and retention practices.

Retention and deletion

We keep account information while your account is active. After you confirm account deletion in the app, access is blocked immediately. The disabled account and progress, including last-used and deletion timestamps, are retained for 30 days and then scheduled for erasure from the active account database. Signing in does not cancel the request.

In-app deletion is available in You → Settings → Delete my account. You can also initiate a request without installing the app by emailing ayushis2k10@gmail.com with the subject “TEZ HAI account deletion”. We must verify account ownership before action; never send your password or sign-in codes. Sending an email alone does not immediately block an account.

The requesting device clears its account copy. Offline devices may retain a copy until they reconnect using the updated app or their local data is cleared. Guest data is separate. Uninstalling the app does not delete a cloud account. Removal from historical analytics providers, infrastructure logs and backups requires the applicable provider process.

Your choices and contact

You can play locally, change notification and measurement choices, sign out or switch players, and request access, correction or deletion by contacting ayushis2k10@gmail.com. We may need to verify ownership before sharing or changing account information. No service can guarantee absolute security; protect your password and device.

We will update this policy when our practices change and display the revised date. Rights provided by applicable law are unaffected by this notice.

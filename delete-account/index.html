Delete Account

Request deletion of your TEZ HAI? account and associated game data.

Effective: 2026-10-28 · Ayushi Srivastava

Without installing the app

Email ayushis2k10@gmail.com with the subject “TEZ HAI account deletion”. Include your username or the email used for Google or Apple sign-in (including an Apple Hide My Email address, if applicable) so we can locate the account. Never send your password or sign-in codes. We must verify account ownership before deletion; a username or score alone is not proof of ownership. Sending an email does not immediately block the account.

Inside the app

Open You → Settings → Delete my account, sign in if required, and confirm the deletion request. Once deletion is confirmed, the account, sign-in identity and associated game records are removed from the active account database. You can return using the same sign-in method to create a fresh account. Deleted progress is not restored. Restricted records needed to finish provider erasure or authorization revocation may remain until that work is complete. An existing account restricted by age can use Delete account from its restriction screen.

Device data and other copies

Local guest progress is separate and can be removed in Settings or by clearing app storage. Other offline devices may retain account data until reconnecting or clearing local storage. Uninstalling alone does not delete a cloud account. See the Privacy Policy for provider logs, backups and retained analytics.

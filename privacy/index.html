Privacy Policy

How TEZ HAI? handles your information on Android and iOS.

Effective: 2026-10-27 · Ayushi Srivastava

Who runs TEZ HAI?

TEZ HAI? (com.tezhai.app) is published by Ayushi Srivastava. For support, privacy questions, or requests about your information, email ayushis2k10@gmail.com.

Local play and cloud accounts

Players aged 18 or older can play without creating an account. Your nickname, puzzle progress, unfinished boards, settings and recorded play time are saved on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data can remove local information that has not been synced.

If you create a cloud account, we process an account identifier, username or sign-in identity, age band (and a guardian-permission declaration if retained from an earlier teen account), completed puzzle records, scores, skips, hints, mistakes, recorded play time, completion dates and the iOS or Android platform recorded for each new result. The platform and completion time show where you last played; they do not identify a particular handset. We record the latest online account activity and security/session information needed to operate the account. Exact age and date of birth are not saved as profile fields.

Supabase provides authentication and cloud storage. Username accounts use an internal sign-in address, not a recovery email. Passwords are handled by the authentication service; plaintext passwords are not stored with game results. Google and Apple sign-in share the identity information needed to authenticate you, which may include your email address, name and profile information. Username password recovery by email is currently unavailable.

Completed local results are imported into an eligible cloud account only when you choose to save them to that account. Remembered account sessions are stored in device-only secure storage so you can switch accounts without entering credentials each time; signing out removes the current remembered session. Unfinished boards remain on the device. Cloud saving does not promise to resume the same unfinished board on every device.

Why we process information

We process account identity and saved game records to provide the account and synchronization service you request. We process optional usage analytics and optional audience counts only after your consent. You can withdraw consent for future analytics in Settings → Privacy choices without losing access to the game.

We process necessary security, support and privacy-choice records to operate the service, protect accounts, prevent abuse and honor your privacy decisions. Where applicable law uses legal bases, these activities rely on performing our agreement with you, our legitimate interests in operating a secure service, or a legal obligation, as appropriate. A notification permission does not authorize gameplay analytics.

Age eligibility: 18 and older

TEZ HAI is intended for people aged 18 or older. The updated app asks a new local player for their age before play and retains only an age group, not the exact age or date of birth. An under-18 answer blocks gameplay and new cloud-account registration. A guardian checkbox does not provide access. The age entry is a declaration, not verification of identity.

Earlier versions allowed local child profiles and some teen cloud accounts. The updated app restricts those profiles from gameplay, progress uploads, analytics and reminders. Earlier saved data is not automatically destroyed by this restriction. Existing account holders can still reach account help, privacy information, deletion and an adult-age correction review. An existing teen account cannot promote itself to adult by entering a different age. Contact support to request an age correction or deletion. Do not send identity documents in feedback or chat; support will explain any necessary verification separately.

Older installed versions cannot be changed remotely in every respect, including offline local play. Update the app to receive these controls. If you believe a child has provided information, contact ayushis2k10@gmail.com so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

An adult may separately choose to add their broad age range to combined audience counts. This is off by default and does not include an account ID, nickname or exact age; counts are not a measure of unique people. The request still involves the technical network processing described below.

Device reminders

After an eligible adult allows device notifications, the app schedules local reminders on this phone in India Standard Time (Asia/Kolkata). They do not require an account, push token or internet connection. Messages rotate through the approved catalog. The operating system limits pending reminders, so the app replenishes the schedule when it opens. Reminders are removed when this app detects that the current player is not eligible. You can disable notifications in phone settings. Delivery, sound and vibration remain subject to operating-system settings, Focus modes and battery restrictions.

Notification permission does not grant analytics consent. For adults who separately enable optional analytics, we record permission outcomes, schedule setup and reminder taps. We do not claim to measure delivery when the app is closed. This version retires older account-linked push registrations when the associated account is available online.

Optional usage analytics

Optional usage analytics is off by default. Eligible players see an optional choice on first launch on iOS and Android, including after an app update if this player has no saved decision. Yes and No thanks both let you play. Existing choices are retained across app updates. Adult players can also change this choice in Settings → Privacy choices. When enabled, Google Analytics for Firebase and PostHog receive screen names, game starts and outcomes, levels, scores, hints, mistakes, duration, account-flow outcomes, sharing and backup actions, settings changes, feedback submission outcomes, and reminder interactions. Cloud analytics uses the account identifier; local analytics uses a random identifier stored on this phone. We do not send nicknames, emails, passwords, puzzle boards, feedback text or precise location as event properties. Device platform, app version and technical SDK information may accompany events. Advertising IDs, advertising consent, session replay and automatic screen capture are disabled. Firebase may derive approximate location from the network IP address; the app does not request location permission. PostHog IP-based location enrichment is disabled.

Analytics consent is separate for each player on this phone and is not inherited when switching accounts. Turning it off or signing out stops new tracking and clears that player’s pending event queue on the phone. Opted-in offline events are held for at most seven days, up to 200 events, and sent when eligible and connected. Turning analytics off does not automatically erase events already received by providers; contact support for access or deletion requests. Provider security logs, backups and retained analytics follow their applicable settings and service terms.

The game contains no advertisements, paid subscriptions or in-app purchases. The public legal site has no analytics or tracking cookies.

Sharing and device access

When an eligible player chooses to share, the app creates a result image and opens the operating system’s share sheet. You choose the destination and what to send. The receiving app’s practices apply to information you share; copies retained by recipients are outside our control.

The game plays audio but does not record your microphone. Photos access may be requested by the sharing functionality when you explicitly choose a related action. The notification permission prompt is shown separately after the eligible player has finished the initial privacy choice.

Service providers and technical information

Supabase processes account and game records. Google and Apple process their respective sign-in flows. Feedback you choose to send is forwarded to the publisher’s support inbox through the configured email provider. Network identifiers are hashed for feedback rate limiting; feedback text is not included in application logs. GitHub Pages serves these public information pages. The legal site does not include analytics, replay, advertising, account login or tracking cookies.

Network providers necessarily process information such as IP addresses, request times and technical logs to deliver and secure requests. Providers may process information outside your country. Their security logs and backups follow their applicable service terms and retention practices.

Retention and deletion

We keep account information while your account is active. After you confirm account deletion in the app, access is blocked immediately. The disabled account and progress, including last-used and deletion timestamps, are retained for 30 days and then scheduled for erasure from the active account database. Signing in does not cancel the request.

In-app deletion is available in You → Settings → Delete my account. You can also initiate a request without installing the app by emailing ayushis2k10@gmail.com with the subject “TEZ HAI account deletion”. We must verify account ownership before action; never send your password or sign-in codes. Sending an email alone does not immediately block an account.

The requesting device clears its account copy. Offline devices may retain a copy until they reconnect using the updated app or their local data is cleared. Guest data is separate. Uninstalling the app does not delete a cloud account. Removal from historical analytics providers, infrastructure logs and backups requires the applicable provider process.

Your choices and contact

You can play locally, change notification permission in your phone settings, sign out or switch players, and contact ayushis2k10@gmail.com to request access, correction, deletion or a copy of your information. Depending on your location, you may also have rights to restrict or object to processing, transfer your data, withdraw consent, or complain to your local privacy authority. We may need to verify ownership before sharing or changing account information. We will respond under the requirements and time limits that apply to your request. No service can guarantee absolute security; protect your password and device.

We will update this policy when our practices change and display the revised date. Rights provided by applicable law are unaffected by this notice.

Privacy-choice receipts and feedback

We record when the optional analytics choice is shown and whether you allow or decline it. These operational receipts include a random player/device-context reference, choice, policy version, date, platform, and account ID for a signed-in account. They are kept separately from gameplay analytics, including when analytics is declined. They help us honor choices and understand consent completion. They do not authorize behavioral tracking. New receipts do not reconstruct choices from previous installs.

Feedback you submit is stored privately in our support database and may be forwarded to our support email. It is not sent as event text to Firebase or PostHog. Include contact information only if you want a reply. Feedback database records are retained for up to 90 days and privacy receipts for up to one year, with expired records removed by daily maintenance; linked database records are removed when your cloud account is erased. Copies delivered to the support inbox may remain for resolving your request and necessary records; contact support to request their removal as well.

Optional analytics uses random session, puzzle-attempt, notification-interaction and share-operation IDs to connect steps without recording message contents, contacts, nicknames or device hardware identifiers. Notification permission is separate from optional analytics.

Apple authorization and account deletion

For new Apple sign-ins, the authorization code is exchanged on our server and the resulting revocation credential is encrypted in a restricted vault. It is used to disconnect Apple authorization when account deletion is requested, then removed. Credentials awaiting revocation survive account purging until the revocation attempt succeeds. An older Apple account without a stored credential can still request deletion; the app explains how to disconnect TEZ HAI in Apple Account settings.

Account Help

Get back to your TEZ HAI? journey.

Effective: 2026-10-27 · Ayushi Srivastava

Google sign-in

Use the same Google account you used before. If you forgot its password, use Google’s account recovery service.

Username sign-in

Username passwords cannot currently be reset. Keep your password securely saved. Creating a new account does not recover the old journey. Support cannot bypass account ownership checks.

Contact support

Email ayushis2k10@gmail.com for help. Never send passwords or sign-in codes.

Apple sign-in on iOS

Use Continue with Apple with the same Apple Account you used before. If you selected Hide My Email, keep using Apple sign-in for that journey. Never share an Apple password, token or sign-in code with support.

Age restrictions and corrections

TEZ HAI is now for players aged 18 or older. Existing teen accounts retain access to privacy information and account deletion. If the recorded age group is incorrect or you have since turned 18, use Request an adult age review or contact ayushis2k10@gmail.com. Do not send identity documents through general feedback.

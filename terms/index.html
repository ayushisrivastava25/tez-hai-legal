Publisher and purpose

TEZ HAI? is published by Ayushi Srivastava. Support: ayushis2k10@gmail.com. The game provides puzzles for entertainment and practice. TEZ scores describe game activity, not a scientific measurement of intelligence or a guarantee of cognitive improvement.

Players and accounts

Under-13 players use local device profiles. Players aged 13–17 may create cloud accounts only after declaring parent or guardian permission and supervision; otherwise they may play locally. Adults may create independent cloud accounts. The permission declaration is not identity verification.

Your journey

Keep sign-in details secure and switch players before sharing your device. Username password recovery is currently unavailable. Local progress can be lost when the app is removed or its data is cleared. Cloud synchronization requires an internet connection. Completed results can sync; unfinished boards remain local.

Fair use

Do not access another person’s account, interfere with other players, circumvent account protections, or deliberately disrupt the service. Please report technical or security problems to support without sending passwords or sign-in codes.

Account deletion

You can request account deletion in the app after signing in or contact support without installing the app. Confirmed in-app deletion blocks access immediately; active account data is scheduled for erasure after 30 days. Email requests require ownership verification before action. The Privacy Policy explains local device copies and provider retention.
